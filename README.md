# texi - Mobile Textbook Store App

## Overview

### What Does It Do
texi is a mobile-first Android Textbook Store App that enables students to communicate, buy, and sell second-hand textbooks within a trusted institutional environment. It provides a centralised and effective solution for students needing affordable prescribed textbooks. The app is lightweight and designed to operate efficiently with limited mobile data.

texi is designed for undergraduate students in higher education institutions who are seeking affordable alternatives to purchasing new prescribed textbooks or who want to sell their previously owned textbooks.

### What Does It Solve
Many students experience challenges accessing affordable prescribed textbooks. This forces them to rely on informal and unprotected forms of communication. Since textbooks are updated frequently, it is difficult to find the correct edition, and some are only available in print. Students often miss important coursework because of suppliers being out of stock or delayed deliveries. Some solutions exist; however, they are often fragmented and inefficient. This makes it harder for students to find reliable or trustworthy sellers and leaves students vulnerable to phishing and scams. Since existing solutions are not centralised and most universities have different prescribed materials, students must research and contact multiple sellers to get the textbooks they need.

### Included Features
- User registration and login
- Browse and view textbook listings
- Upload second-hand textbooks for sale
- Search and filter textbooks
- Buyers can inquire about textbooks

### Tech Stack
- Android Studio  
- Kotlin
- XML
